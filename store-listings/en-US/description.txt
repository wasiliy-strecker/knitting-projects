Knitting Projects keeps your knitting and crochet ideas together. Record a project name, yarn or material, color, storage location and personal notes. Add one optional photo and choose Planned, Started or Finished.

Search all five text fields, edit projects and keep track of their status. JPEG and PNG photos are stored with each project. Your original image files stay unchanged.

Your collection stays on your computer. No account or internet connection is required. The interface is available in German, English, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese (Brazil). Language changes apply the next time you start the app.
